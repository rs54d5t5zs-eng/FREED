The origin

Born from three years of studying two financial worlds

"What if the most robust financial principles from one system could be applied anywhere, by anyone?"

For over three years, I've been fascinated by how different financial systems approach the same fundamental question: how do you make money work ethically, fairly, and productively?

Islamic finance and conventional finance answer this question in radically different ways. One forbids fixed interest and demands real risk-sharing. The other is built entirely on it. Both have strengths. Both have blind spots.

Freed is what happens when you take the most compelling mechanism from Islamic finance — the Moucharaka Moutanaqissa — and adapt it into a participative finance model that works within French law, for anyone, regardless of background.

No interest. No artificial guarantee. A genuine partnership between those who have capital and those who have projects.

System 1
Conventional Finance
Built on interest — the lender is always protected regardless of the borrower's outcome. Capital grows whether the project succeeds or fails. The risk is asymmetric by design.
Interest-based · Fixed return · Bank as intermediary
System 2
Islamic Finance
Built on real economic activity. Money must be tied to a tangible project. Profit is legitimate — but only when risk is genuinely shared between all parties. The Moucharaka Moutanaqissa is its most elegant instrument.
Risk-sharing · Profit-based · No fixed interest
Freed
The synthesis
Inspired by the teachings of the Moucharaka Moutanaqissa, adapted into a French participative finance framework. No religious label. No jargon. Just the mechanism — equity, transparency, shared stakes.
Open to everyone · Ethical · French law
The mechanism

A degressive sharing contract — step by step

The entrepreneur gradually buys back the investors' shares over the contract period. At the end, they are the sole owner. No debt. No interest. A real partnership with a clear exit.

①
Submission
The entrepreneur submits their project — amount needed, duration, profit-sharing rate.
②
Validation
Freed reviews the application. Rigorous due diligence before any publication.
③
SPV created
A dedicated company (SPV) is created with its own bank account and IBAN.
④
Collection
The community invests from 50€. Each investor becomes a co-shareholder.
⑤
Sharing
Monthly profit distributions. Progressive buyback of shares. Full ownership at maturity.
All-or-nothing model: if the funding target is not reached within the deadline, every investor is fully refunded. Freed receives nothing. Risk is shared at every level.
Key concepts

Every term, clearly explained

From the founding principle to the technical infrastructure — what each concept means and why it matters.

⚖
Founding Principle
Moucharaka Moutanaqissa
A centuries-old Islamic financial contract meaning "degressive partnership." The investor gradually transfers ownership to the entrepreneur over the contract period, as profits are shared monthly.
Freed adapts its core logic — shared risk, shared profit, progressive buyback — into French participative finance law.
🏛
Legal Structure
SPV — Special Purpose Vehicle
A dedicated company created exclusively for one project. It has its own legal identity, its own bank account, its own shareholders. If this project fails, no other project or Freed SAS is affected.
Think of it as a separate drawer for each project — if one drawer catches fire, the others remain intact.
📋
Contract Type
Société en participation
The French legal form used by Freed (Art. 1871–1873 of the French Civil Code). A partnership without legal registration, allowing multiple people to associate around a project and share profits and losses.
This is the legal backbone that makes the degressive sharing contract valid and enforceable in France.
🏦
Payment Infrastructure
Swan — IBAN per SPV
Swan is a French Banking-as-a-Service provider licensed by the Banque de France. It allows Freed to open a real French IBAN (FR76...) for each SPV — so investors wire directly to the project's account.
Money never mixes between projects. Total transparency. This is the technological backbone of Freed's architecture.
🔑
Regulatory Status
IFP — ORIAS registration
Intermédiaire en Financement Participatif. A mandatory registration with the French financial regulator ORIAS to legally operate a participative finance platform. Not a banking license — an intermediary status.
This is what allows Freed to legally match investors and entrepreneurs without holding funds itself.
✍
Digital Signing
Yousign — eIDAS certified
Every degressive sharing contract between Freed, the entrepreneur, and each investor is digitally signed before any funds are released. Yousign is a French solution certified under the EU eIDAS regulation.
Each party receives a legally binding signed copy before any money moves.
Case study

Thomas, his VTC license, and 200 strangers who believed in him

Thomas has been a ride-hailing driver for 3 years. He rents his car for 350€/week — 18,200€/year gone. With his own vehicle, he doubles his net margin. He needs 20,000€.

The full scenario
From the first euro collected to full ownership — every step

The collection

Thomas submits 21,000€ on Freed (20,000€ net + 5% platform fee)
150 investors put in an average of 140€ each
Sophie, a nurse, invests 600€ → she holds 3% of the SPV
Contract: 36 months · 25% of net monthly profits shared
A dedicated IBAN (FR76 002...) is opened for this SPV only
All-or-nothing: if 21,000€ isn't raised, everyone is refunded

Sophie's returns — month by month

Month 6 · net profit 2,200€Sophie receives 16.50€
Month 12 · net profit 2,500€Sophie receives 18.75€
Month 24 · net profit 2,800€Sophie receives 21.00€
Month 36 · final settlementSophie receives 21.00€
Sophie's total1,230€ for 600€ invested (+105%)
Thomas saves 18,200€/year in rental fees. Over 3 years: 54,600€ saved. He owns his vehicle outright. No interest ever paid.

What if Thomas has a bad month?

This is where Freed is honest. If Thomas generates zero net profit in a given month, Sophie receives nothing that month — no profit distribution, no share buyback. The risk is genuinely shared. No one is protected at someone else's expense. That is a real partnership, not a disguised financial product.

Infrastructure

How money flows without going through Freed

Freed never holds user funds. A Banque de France-licensed payment provider handles everything — escrow, transfers, compliance.

Recommended · Scale phase
Swan ★
French Banking-as-a-Service provider licensed by the Banque de France. Opens a real French IBAN per SPV — investors wire directly to the project's dedicated account. Money never mixes between projects.
Sophie wires her 600€ to FR76 002... (Thomas's SPV). Total transparency. This is the architectural ideal for Freed.
MVP · Fast integration
Mangopay
Crowdfunding-specialized electronic money institution. Mature REST API. Handles escrow during collection, automatic refunds if target not reached, and monthly distribution payouts.
3-4 weeks integration. The fastest path to validating the model on early projects.
Alternative to Mangopay
Lemonway
ACPR-licensed electronic money institution. Lower cost than Mangopay, IFP-certified. Well-documented API. Strong track record with French participative finance platforms.
Good option for the growth phase before migrating to Swan's dedicated IBAN architecture.
Digital signing
Yousign
French eIDAS-certified digital signature solution. Every sharing contract is signed by all parties before any funds are released. Each signatory receives a legally binding copy.
No money moves without a signed contract. This is the legal and ethical backbone of every transaction.
Mandatory regulatory status
IFP · ORIAS
Intermédiaire en Financement Participatif. Mandatory ORIAS registration to legally operate a participative finance platform in France. An intermediary status — not a banking license.
This is what legally allows Freed to connect investors and entrepreneurs without holding or managing funds itself.
Legal foundation
Société en participation
French Civil Code Articles 1871–1873. A partnership without formal registration that allows multiple parties to associate around a project and share profits and losses proportionally.
The legal instrument that translates the degressive sharing mechanism into enforceable French law.

Freed earns nothing if a collection fails. Our incentives are perfectly aligned with every user on the platform.
